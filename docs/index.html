Building reliable perception for real-world systems.

Hi, I'm Jeong-min Hong, an AI & Robotics undergraduate researcher at Kwangwoon University.

I work across robot vision, learning-based recognition, and robotic system prototyping. My approach is to stay with lightweight, existing models and representations and make them robust and accurate, so that systems keep working with noisy, incomplete, and changing observations. I am currently most interested in robustness and embodied AI.

01 / About

From algorithms to working systems

I enjoy research that connects the mathematical and algorithmic side of robotics with real-world implementation. My interests span computer vision, robot navigation, embedded inference, and human-centered robotic systems.

Across my projects, a recurring theme is the gap between a model that works under ideal inputs and a system that remains dependable outside the lab.

Research direction

Making AI systems more dependable

Robust perception under missing or corrupted data, reliable action-level reasoning, and practical edge deployment.
