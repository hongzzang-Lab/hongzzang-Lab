# Jeong-min Hong

**AI & Robotics Undergraduate Researcher · Kwangwoon University**

I am an undergraduate researcher working at the intersection of **robot perception, computer vision, and edge AI**. I enjoy building complete robotic systems—from interpreting sensor observations and developing learning-based models to deploying perception and decision-making pipelines on hardware.

My research interests are centered on a practical question: **How can intelligent systems remain reliable when observations are incomplete, noisy, or changing?**

I am particularly interested in assistive and safety-oriented robotics, real-world perception, and autonomous systems.

## Publications & Research

### Published journal paper

**Hong Jeongmin et al. (2026).**  
*긴급상황 속 수어해석 애플리케이션*  
*Journal of the Institute of Electronics and Information Engineers*, **63**(1), 81–88. **First author.**

[Paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.5573/ieie.2026.63.1.81) · [Research journey](https://github.com/hongzzang-Lab/sign-language-landmark-research)

A landmark-based Korean sign language interpretation application for emergency communication, using 347-dimensional derived features and a Bi-LSTM recognition model. The paper reports **90.00% accuracy** and **89.11% F1-score** under its evaluation protocol.

### Conference research

**Hong Jeongmin & Lee Yae Sop (2026).**  
*Landmark 기반 수어 인식에서 특징 조합에 따른 성능 및 입력 변형 민감도 비교*  
**CICS'26**, Special Session 7 (SS07-07).

[Official conference program](https://cics.kiee.or.kr/pages/special_session.vm) · [Study overview](https://github.com/hongzzang-Lab/sign-language-landmark-research/blob/main/docs/cics-2026.md)

Compared 153D–347D landmark-derived representations across recognition architectures and input perturbations. The study examined when additional features help—and when they increase sensitivity to degraded observations.

**Ongoing:** Missing-landmark reconstruction, derived-feature distortion, and downstream recognition analysis. Details are documented at a high level in the [sign-language research portfolio](https://github.com/hongzzang-Lab/sign-language-landmark-research).
